Good questions

          
You don't need to speak fluent Disney before you call.

        


        

          

            Can Jordan help if we do not know which Disney destination to choose?
            
Yes. Destination matching is part of the value: group, budget, travel tolerance, interests, trip length, and the rest of the vacation can all change which Disney experience makes the most sense.

          

          

            Is this only for families with children?
            
No. The site is intentionally designed for families, couples, adults-only trips, friends, solo travelers, celebrations, and multi-generational groups.

          

          

            Can Jordan help with Disney outside the United States?
            
That is a core part of the brand direction. The goal is worldwide Disney vacation planning, including Disney destinations that are part of larger international itineraries.

          

          

            Can the plan include more than Disney?
            
Yes. A strong international trip may use Disney as one anchor while also making room for the city, country, cruise, beach, rail route, or other experiences around it.
