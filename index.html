Good to know

Before you fall in love with a number.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Published prices are snapshots of official public pages and may change. Availability, taxes, exchange rates, eligibility and reservation requirements can change the final cost.

Why not scrape live airfare directly into the page?

This site runs on static GitHub Pages. Reliable live airfare and hotel inventory needs a licensed API or backend service. Instead, the site links to live comparison tools and lets you enter the live quote into the budget.

Can Jordan book every destination shown here?

The site is designed around worldwide Disney planning. Jordan’s final supplier/agency affiliations and booking permissions should be confirmed before presenting any specific credential or booking authority.

Is this an official Disney website?

No. This is an independent travel-planning website. Disney names and destination names are used only to identify the travel products being discussed.
